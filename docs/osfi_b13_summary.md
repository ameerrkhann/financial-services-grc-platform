# OSFI B-13 — Technology and Cyber Risk Management
## Quick Reference Summary
**Effective:** January 1, 2024  
**Applies to:** All federally regulated financial institutions in Canada

---

## The Three Domains

B-13 is organised into three domains, each setting out key components for
sound risk management:

| # | Domain | Focus |
|---|--------|-------|
| 1 | Governance and Risk Management | Accountability, risk appetite, policy, roles |
| 2 | Technology Operations and Resilience | Asset management, change, incident and problem management, disaster recovery |
| 3 | Cyber Security | Identify, defend, detect, respond and recover from cyber threats |

---

## The 10 Key Expectations

| # | Requirement | What It Means |
|---|------------|---------------|
| 1 | Board ownership | Board formally owns and oversees cyber risk |
| 2 | Risk appetite | Written statement of acceptable cyber risk levels |
| 3 | Continuous assessment | Ongoing asset inventory, threat and vulnerability management |
| 4 | Critical systems | Identify and prioritise protection of critical systems |
| 5 | Control validation | Controls must be tested, not just documented |
| 6 | Incident reporting | Reportable technology and cyber incidents reported to OSFI within 24 hours. **The 24-hour requirement is set by OSFI's separate *Technology and Cyber Security Incident Reporting* advisory**, not by B-13 |
| 7 | Third-party risk | Banks accountable for vendor-introduced cyber risk |
| 8 | Cloud and third-party technology | Cloud services assessed for concentration, access and location of data. B-13 does **not** impose a Canadian data-residency rule; B-10 sets the expectation that a FRFI knows and controls where its data is stored and accessed |
| 9 | Operational resilience | Critical operations maintained during cyber incidents |
| 10 | Continuous improvement | Cyber risk maturity must improve over time with a roadmap |

## Key Terms

- **FRFI** — Federally Regulated Financial Institution (who B-13 applies to)
- **Technology risk** — risk of loss from technology failures or inadequacies
- **Cyber risk** — risk of loss from malicious digital attacks or unauthorised access
- **Risk appetite** — the level of risk an organisation is willing to accept
- **Operational resilience** — ability to deliver critical services through disruption (E-21 is the guideline that sets this out in full)
- **Reportable incident** — a technology or cyber incident meeting OSFI's reporting threshold, which must be reported within 24 hours
